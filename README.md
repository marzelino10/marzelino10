## 🧭 A little context

I’m **Marzelino**, an analytics engineer and former BI team lead who likes making complicated things useful. At **VACO, supporting Google Surveys in Taipei**, I built centralized SQL reporting, automated operational workflows, and mentored analysts.

Today, I bring that same approach to **data pipelines, dashboards, and business applications**—from cloud data models to tools people use in the field and at the counter. An **MBA and a Chemical Engineering background** shape how I work: understand the process, question the numbers, and build something practical.
