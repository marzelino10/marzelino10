## 🧭 From the numbers to the workflow

I’m **Marzelino**, an analytics engineer and former **BI Team Lead at VACO, supporting Google Surveys in Taipei**. My work connects three things: **reliable data, useful reporting, and the business processes behind them**.

I build SQL/dbt models and cloud pipelines, translate business questions into Power BI reports, and develop operational tools for approvals, inventory, billing, and customer follow-ups. That includes a **30-project Google Workspace automation portfolio**, a construction operations PWA with accounting integration, and an offline desktop POS.

My focus is practical: **make the data trustworthy, make the next action clear, and make the system recover when something goes wrong**. An MBA and a Chemical Engineering background bring both business context and a process-oriented approach.

## 🔎 How I build

- **Business rules belong in the system.** Consent gates before customer emails, explicit review for ambiguous data, and server-side permission checks.
- **Retries should be safe.** Stable command IDs, duplicate guards, staged syncs, and resumable workflows protect against repeated or interrupted work.
- **Numbers should be explainable.** Raw evidence, versioned transformations, stock ledgers, and historical cost snapshots make corrections traceable.
